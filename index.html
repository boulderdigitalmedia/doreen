How it works

Three steps to something they'll never forget

Step 1
Write your notes
Add as many reasons as you like. Pair each one with a photo. They'll get one delivered each day, week, or month.
Step 2
Share their link
Every gift gets a personal URL. Send it to your recipient — they open it once and choose how they want to be notified.
Step 3
Let it run
A Note For You handles the rest. A new note lands for them every send day, automatically. You can add more notes anytime.
Everything included

Built for the long game

Push notifications
Recipients install the app to their home screen and get a notification each send day — no App Store required.
Email delivery
Beautiful HTML emails with the note and photo, linking back to the full app.
SMS add-on
Enable text message delivery for recipients who prefer it. Their number stays private — collected during setup.
Photos with every note
Attach a photo to any note. A memory, a candid, a screenshot of something that made you think of them.
Your cadence, your call
Send daily, weekly, every two weeks, or monthly. Change it any time from your dashboard.
2 gifts included, add more any time
Every subscription includes 2 gifts — a partner, a parent, a friend, a child. Need more people on your list? Add extra gifts for $2/mo or $20/yr, matching your plan.
